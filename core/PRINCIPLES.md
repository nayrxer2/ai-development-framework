# Principles

1. **Clear accountability:** Every deliverable has one accountable owner, even when multiple roles contribute.
2. **Explicit authority:** Roles make decisions only within their defined authority and escalate consequential ambiguity.
3. **Platform independence:** Core framework knowledge remains usable across models, editors, and agent runtimes.
4. **Progressive context:** Load only the skills and references relevant to the current work.
5. **Evidence-based completion:** Report checks and outcomes accurately; do not claim unverified work is complete.
6. **Independent verification:** High-risk work receives review appropriate to its risk, separate from implementation ownership.
7. **Traceable change:** Significant decisions, approvals, handoffs, and changes remain understandable after the task.
8. **Least complexity:** Prefer the simplest structure and process that reliably meets the need.
9. **Human authority:** Product direction and explicitly reserved approvals remain with the designated human decision owner.
