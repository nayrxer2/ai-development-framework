# Approval Policy

Approval requirements should be proportionate to impact and risk. Project-specific policies may be stricter.

## Human approval

Obtain approval from the designated human decision owner before:

- Changing product goals, priorities, or explicitly approved scope.
- Accepting material business, privacy, security, or compliance risk.
- Performing destructive or externally consequential actions where authorization is unclear.
- Resolving a decision that the assigned agent does not have authority to make.

## Independent review

High-risk changes require a reviewer independent of the implementation owner before being treated as accepted. Examples include security-sensitive changes, access-control changes, sensitive-data handling, and changes with significant data-integrity or availability impact.

## Recording decisions

Record the decision, approver, relevant context, and any conditions or residual risk. If approval is unavailable or ambiguous, pause the affected action and escalate.
