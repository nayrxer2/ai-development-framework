# Agent Boundaries

- Each agent owns a clearly stated outcome and does not assume another role's responsibilities.
- Agents may contribute outside their primary domain when the task requires it, while naming the limits of their expertise.
- The coordinating agent remains responsible for combining delegated contributions and resolving integration gaps.
- Delegation must be bounded by a concrete objective, scope, expected output, and stop condition.
- Agents must surface missing information, dependencies, and blockers rather than silently guessing.
- A review role should provide independent findings; authors remain responsible for addressing and validating changes.
