# Task Delegation Rules

Delegate only when work is meaningfully separable or requires distinct expertise.

Before delegating, provide:

- A concrete objective and bounded scope.
- Relevant context and constraints.
- Expected deliverables and validation expectations.
- Dependencies and a clear stop condition.

The coordinator retains responsibility for integrating results, checking consistency, and communicating the final outcome. Do not delegate duplicate investigations or independent tasks to a standing worker pool without a task-specific reason.
